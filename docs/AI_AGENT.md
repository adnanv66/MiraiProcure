# Mirai AI Agent Specifications — MiraiProcure (未来プロキュア)

## AI Capabilities
- **ASK:** Procurement QA and spend analytics lookup.
- **EXECUTE:** Authorizes server-side tools (`createPR`, `createRFQ`, `analyzeQuotation`, `recommendVendor`, `createPO`).
- **ANALYZE:** Landed cost calculation & multi-factor scoring.
- **PREDICT:** Stockout forecasting and reorder recommendation.
- **MONITOR:** Risk radar and price anomaly detection.
- **GENERATE:** Purchase Order draft generation.
- **RECOMMEND:** Explainable decision recommendations.

## Autonomy Modes
- **Level 1 (Assist):** AI recommends only.
- **Level 2 (Semi-Autonomous):** AI executes low-risk actions, human confirms high-value actions.
- **Level 3 (Governed Autonomous):** AI runs approved routine workflows.
